The source interpretation is discussed in [SCOPE.md](SCOPE.md). The later diameter restriction is evidence of a proper convention, not proof that the original authors excluded paths. The 2015 balance convention also permits centre degrees differing by one, while the original parenthetical uses equal-sized stars. Covering every proper double comet accommodates either balance convention.

## Coverage and result

The review inspected the existing search log and returned citation titles for the AHZ paper: 21 OpenAlex, 34 Semantic Scholar, and 4 zbMATH records; and for the 2012 paper: 4 OpenAlex and 9 Semantic Scholar records. These overlapping counts must not be added as distinct publications. Relevant nearby full texts and primary abstracts were checked, not every citing paper in full.

Fresh searches combined the authors' names with Randić/Randic, algebraic connectivity, product, double comet/double-comet, threshold, 13/14, correction, and counterexample. No earlier result with the precise finite product comparisons or an earlier product-threshold correction was located in those sources.

**Conclusion:** no prior result located in this review. This is weaker than proving novelty, certifying the conjecture was still open, or completing historical-priority clearance. No MathSciNet subscription search was performed; incomplete indexing and inaccessible texts remain limitations. The published theorem is stated independently of any novelty claim.
